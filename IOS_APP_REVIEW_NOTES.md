# iOS App Review Notes — Version 1.0 (59)

Falora is designed as an interactive Turkish cultural fortune workshop rather
than a passive catalog of generic horoscope reports.

## Meaningfully different iOS experience

- Users actively perform each ritual: scattering beans, shaping water symbols,
  selecting tarot or playing cards, and uploading both palms or a complete
  coffee-cup set.
- Coffee, bean and tarot rituals can be sent to named human interpreters,
  Serdar or Hatice. Their availability and daily capacity are shown in the app.
- The dedicated Relationship Workshop contains two distinct tools: a two-person
  compatibility study and situation-specific relationship guidance.
- Previous sessions remain in a personal archive with their selected symbols,
  cards, interpreter, status and completed interpretation.
- The iPad interface uses an adaptive workshop layout rather than stretching a
  phone category list.

## Suggested review path

1. Open **Tombik Teyze Fal Atölyesi** on the Home screen.
2. Choose **Bakla Falı** and interact with the bean-scattering ritual.
3. Choose **Tarot** to select cards directly, then view the interpreter choice.
4. Open **İlişki Atölyesi** and inspect both **Çift Uyumu** and
   **İlişki Tavsiyesi**.
5. Open **Fallarım** to see how sessions are retained as an ongoing archive.

The astrology, numerology and daily horoscope material is supplementary and is
not the primary iOS navigation or value proposition.
